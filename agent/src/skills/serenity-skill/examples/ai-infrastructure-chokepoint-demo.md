# Demo: AI Infrastructure Chokepoint Research

> Note: This is a dated example. Always use live sources for current research.

## Prompt

```
Use serenity-skill to deeply research AI infrastructure opportunities.
Map the value chain, find the scarce layers, and rank the top research priorities.
```

## Example answer shape

Start with the layers: optical interconnect, advanced packaging, data-center power, and thermal management. These sit closer to real deployment constraints than the broad AI application layer. They are easier to verify through tangible evidence: orders, margins, certifications, capacity announcements.

## Compact ranking

| Priority | Layer | Why it matters | First evidence to check | What could weaken the view |
|----------|-------|---------------|------------------------|---------------------------|
| 1 | Optical interconnect | Bandwidth is the physical bottleneck; coherent/CPO transition creates supplier change | Customer qualifications, laser/module orders, margin inflection | Faster-than-expected substitution of alternate interconnect approaches |
| 2 | Advanced packaging | HBM and 3D IC are the scaling constraint; few qualified OSATs and equipment suppliers | Capacity reservations, equipment lead times, customer co-investment | Process yield improvement faster than expected; new entrants qualify |
| 3 | Data-center power | Power density is the hard ceiling; transformers, switchgear have multi-year lead times | Utility interconnection agreements, equipment backlogs, component orders | Grid upgrades accelerate; modular power solutions reduce lead times |
| 4 | Thermal management | Liquid cooling moves from optional to required; qualified loops are scarce | Customer qualifications, fluid/component orders, system integrator partnerships | Air cooling improvements extend the transition window |

## Final research move

Verify the top optical interconnect layer first through filings, qualifications, announcements, capacity data, and gross margins. If evidence supports the bottleneck thesis, validate downstream through packaging and power equipment cross-checks.
